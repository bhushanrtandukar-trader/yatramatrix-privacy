# Delete your YatraMatrix account

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

You can delete your account and everything you made with it. There are two ways.

## In the app

1. Open YatraMatrix and tap **Profile**.
2. Tap **Account and privacy**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

This removes your account and everything linked to it: your trips, the prices and reports you shared, and any listing claims.

## Without the app

E-mail **bhushanrtandukar@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my YatraMatrix account". We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What is not removed

Questions sent anonymously from Ask Dawa and anonymous view counts cannot be linked to you, so they are not removed on request. The questions are deleted automatically after 180 days.
