# YatraMatrix privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. This is a plain description of what the app does.

YatraMatrix is a travel guide to Nepal: places, treks, safety information and a guide called Ask Dawa. It is published by Bhushan Tandukar. Questions or requests: b.bhushan.tandukar@gmail.com.

## The short version

- You can use almost everything **without an account**. Treks, places, safety information and Ask Dawa work on the phone and mostly without the internet.
- Your **location stays on your phone.** It is used for the SOS screen, "Follow this trek" and "near me" questions, and is not sent to us.
- We never sell your data, and there are no ads or tracking tools in the app.
- If you create an account, we keep your e-mail, your trips and whatever you choose to share. You can delete all of it inside the app.

## What stays on your phone only

Your saved places and trips (unless you sign in, see below), your chat with Ask Dawa, what you have opened and saved (a small "learns from you" model that picks places for Home, which you can switch off or erase in Profile), your language and theme, and downloaded forecast and exchange-rate copies. Location is read only when you tap something that needs it, and only on the phone.

## What we store if you create an account

Stored with our hosting provider, Supabase:

- **Account:** your e-mail address and a password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text), and a display name, "Traveller" by default.
- **Trips:** when you are signed in, your trips are copied to your account so they follow you to another phone.
- **Things you choose to share:** prices you report, trip reports, trail notes and updates, ratings, check-ins and "report a problem" messages. These are checked by a moderator before others see them, and shown with your display name, not your e-mail.
- **If you claim a business listing:** the business name, your role, phone number and e-mail, a note proving you own it, and the text and photos you add to the listing.

## What we receive without an account

- **Questions Ask Dawa could not answer.** When this is on (it is on by default; switch it off under Profile, "Help Dawa learn"), the text of a question Dawa could not answer is sent anonymously, with a short reason, the app language and the app version, and with e-mail addresses and phone numbers removed on your phone first. No name, account, location or device identifier is sent. We use it to decide what to teach Dawa next and delete it after 180 days. Please do not type personal details into Ask Dawa.
- **Anonymous counts** of how many people viewed a business listing or tapped its call, WhatsApp or website button. No user, device or place is recorded.

## Other services the app talks to

- **Map apps.** YatraMatrix has no map of its own. When you tap "Open in your map app", the place's name and coordinates are handed to the map app already on your phone (for example Google Maps or OsmAnd), which handles them under its own policy.
- **Open-Meteo** for weather forecasts: sent the coordinates of a place or trip stop you are viewing, not your phone's position.
- **ExchangeRate-API** for currency rates: a plain request with no personal information.
- **Links you tap** (Google Maps, WhatsApp, a hotel's website) open in the other app or browser under its own policy.

## Location, notifications and permissions

Location is optional: SOS "Find my position", "Follow this trek" (a notification shows your progress along the trail while the screen is off) and "near me" questions. Notifications are used only for that trek-following notice. You can turn either off in Android settings at any time.

## How long we keep it, and deleting it

- **Delete everything in the app:** Profile, "Account and privacy", Delete account. This removes your account and everything you made with it (trips, shared prices and reports, claims).
- **Without the app:** e-mail b.bhushan.tandukar@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will delete it within 30 days. There is also a page for this: [how to delete your account](delete-account).
- Anonymous questions and counts cannot be matched to you, so they are not removed on request (questions are deleted automatically after 180 days).
- Backups held by our hosting provider age out on its normal schedule.

## Safety information

Altitude, SOS, trek and weather guidance is general and can be out of date. It is not medical advice or a guarantee of safety. Use a licensed guide for high or remote treks and follow local rules.

## Children

YatraMatrix is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Accounts are for adults.

## Security

Everything the app sends uses HTTPS and the database only lets each person read and change their own account data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so and this page will show a new date.
